Starward: Sky Map

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Starward (bundle identifier com.mlastro.skymap), developed and published by Khanh To, handles information in the iOS and iPadOS application and on this website. You can also view the canonical hosted privacy policy at https://khanhto.dev/policy.html.

Summary

Starward is built to work offline and respect your privacy. The app has no user accounts, no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no developer servers. All astronomical calculations run locally on your device.

Information Stored and Used on Your Device

Starward uses device sensors and on-device storage solely to render the night sky and remember your personal observing notes:

  • Approximate Location (Optional). With your permission, Starward reads approximate location (kCLLocationAccuracyKilometer) to orient the sky chart, calculate object altitudes, and determine dark-sky twilight times. Coordinates never leave your device; if declined, the sky defaults to Greenwich until you pick an observing site manually.
  • Motion & Orientation Sensors (Optional). Used on device to match the star chart to where you point your iPhone or iPad.
  • Camera (Optional). When you explicitly enable camera overlay mode, the back camera provides a live visual backdrop behind the sky chart so you can align stars overhead. Video frames are processed live on screen and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Observing Journal, Kit & Sites. Sightings logged in your Observing Journal, saved telescopes/eyepieces/cameras, and custom observing sites are stored strictly on your device via Apple SwiftData.

Apple Maps Site Lookup

When you manually search for a city or look up a saved observing site’s time zone in the Site Editor, queries are sent directly from iOS to Apple Maps (MKLocalSearch / reverse geocoding) under Apple’s privacy policy and are never received or stored by the developer.

What Starward Does Not Collect

  • No account registration, name, email address, or phone number.
  • No third-party advertising networks or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • No third-party telemetry or analytics SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information with third parties.

Your Choices & Data Deletion

  • Grant, restrict, or revoke Location and Camera permissions anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Delete individual journal entries, gear items, or saved sites inside the app, or remove all local app data by uninstalling Starward.

Children’s Privacy

Starward is suitable for all ages (4+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
